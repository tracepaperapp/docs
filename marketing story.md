# The Story Behind Tracepaper: Building SaaS Products with a Hobby Budget

Launching a SaaS-worthy product is an exciting ambition. It’s about creating something robust, scalable, and reliable—something that meets the high expectations of today’s users. But for those of us pursuing this goal as a part-time venture, the challenges are significant. How do you deliver a product that’s available **anywhere, anytime**, while operating on a **hobby budget**?

This was the question that sparked the creation of **Tracepaper**, a tool born out of necessity to tackle the challenges of building SaaS applications as a small team with limited time and resources.

## The Serverless Solution: A Double-Edged Sword

To meet these ambitions, we initially turned to **serverless technology**, specifically AWS. Serverless seemed like the perfect solution. It promised scalability and reliability without the overhead of managing infrastructure. With its **pay-as-you-go model**, it fit perfectly into the constraints of a hobby budget.

However, it didn’t take long before we encountered a new problem: **cognitive load**. While serverless reduces operational overhead, it introduces significant complexity in terms of distributed systems. Concepts like event-driven architecture, retries, eventual consistency, and error handling became daily considerations.

The result? Instead of focusing on the functional domain, we found ourselves bogged down in the technical intricacies of serverless systems. It became clear that serverless, while powerful, wasn’t solving the core problem: the need to **focus on the business domain without getting lost in technical noise**.

## Low-Code: More Than Just Fewer Lines of Code

This realization led us to rethink our approach. The promise of **low-code** isn’t about writing less code—it’s about **minimizing cognitive load**. It’s about making it easier to focus on the **functional aspects** of your application rather than the technical underpinnings.

With this in mind, we created **Tracepaper**, a low-code platform designed to:
1. **Reduce complexity:** By abstracting away the intricacies of serverless development.
2. **Keep model and code aligned:** The model becomes the **single source of truth**, ensuring consistency across the application.
3. **Accelerate deployment:** By generating **deployable serverless applications** directly from the model.

This approach offers a critical advantage: **Changing your code means changing your model.** Or, as we put it, **you must change the model to change the code.** This ensures that your system’s structure remains coherent and maintainable, even as it evolves.

## Tracepaper in Action: A Solution to the Problem

Tracepaper directly addresses the challenges illustrated in the accompanying diagram:
- **"No-time & no-money":** For part-time teams, time and resources are always limited. Tracepaper minimizes the effort required to go from concept to deployment.
- **"Skewed signal-to-noise ratio":** Serverless often results in as much infrastructure code as business logic, shifting focus away from what matters. Tracepaper rebalances this ratio by letting developers focus on the functional domain.
- **Anywhere & Anytime:** Tracepaper leverages serverless technology’s robustness and scalability while abstracting its complexity.

### How It Works
Tracepaper acts as a **code generator** that transforms a high-level model into a fully functional serverless application. By automating much of the work traditionally done by developers, it allows you to:
- Focus on **business logic** rather than infrastructure.
- Maintain alignment between the **model** and the **code**.
- Deliver applications that are **ready to deploy**, with minimal effort.

## Why Tracepaper Matters

Tracepaper is more than just a tool—it’s a philosophy. It’s built on the belief that small teams can deliver **enterprise-grade results** without enterprise-scale resources. By reducing the cognitive load of serverless development, it empowers developers to focus on the problems that matter most.

This isn’t about cutting corners or taking shortcuts. It’s about creating tools that make the hard parts of software development easier, enabling teams to move faster and smarter. At its core, Tracepaper is about clarity, focus, and efficiency.
