---
title: Mental Model
---
A **mental model** is your internal representation of how a system works. In software development and architecture, mental models are the frameworks we use to understand and predict the behavior of the systems we design, build, and maintain.

**Why Mental Models Matter in Tracepaper**
  
Tracepaper encourages you to develop clear and aligned mental models to:

• **Understand your functional domain**: Recognize relationships between concepts, define access patterns, and ensure data consistency.

• **Make better decisions**: A solid mental model helps you reason about trade-offs, edge cases, and system behavior.

• **Collaborate effectively**: Shared mental models among your team ensure alignment and reduce miscommunication.

**Mental Models in Architecture**

When working with Tracepaper, mental models often come into play when:

• Mapping functional concepts to implementation (e.g., how aggregates and commands in a domain-driven design reflect the functional requirements).

• Designing systems like [[CQRS]] architectures or [[GraphQL API]]s.

  
**Shaping Mental Models**

Tracepaper simplifies technical concerns, enabling you to focus on:

• **Access patterns**: How users or systems interact with data. 

• **Data consistency**: How data integrity is maintained across operations. 

• **Relationships**: Identifying which concepts are tightly coupled and which are independent. 

By abstracting technical complexities like [[AWS DynamoDB]] design or [[AWS Cognito]] configuration, Tracepaper allows you to refine your mental model without being bogged down by implementation details.
