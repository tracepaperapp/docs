## What is a DSL?
A **Domain Specific Language (DSL)** is a programming or modeling language designed for a specific domain or purpose. Unlike general-purpose languages, DSLs are tailored to express solutions or configurations in a way that closely aligns with the concepts and workflows of their intended domain. This makes them highly effective for specialized tasks while simplifying the interaction for domain experts.

## DSL in Tracepaper
The **Draftsman Modeling Language**, used in Tracepaper, is an example of a DSL. It is expressed in XML, enabling precise and structured modeling of complex systems while ensuring compatibility with various tools and processes.
